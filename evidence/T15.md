# T15 - Outcome objective: India NF racing points, knock-out odds, car choice
**Model:** Claude Sonnet 5.5 (Jainam's session, branch `next-wave`) · **Date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** review

## Built
- `src/daedalus/outcome.py`: turns race-time samples and a field model into expected racing points using the STEM Racing India NF 2025-26 Competition Regulations.
  - `reaction_points`: C9.5 (105 / 100 / 95 for the top three, then 5 + 90 / (Base - fastest) x (Base - own best), Base = 120% of third fastest, floor 5, DNF 4).
  - `best_of`, `total_time_samples`: best of N reaction races, and total race time = car time + reaction (C1.8). Reaction mean and spread have no default; the caller must supply them.
  - `head_to_head`, `knockout_win_distribution`, `expected_knockout_points`: C9.6 (26 / 52 / 85 / 106 / 135 by rounds won), with DNF handling.
  - `cvar`: tail-risk race time.
  - `expected_racing_outcome`: reaction + knock-out + fastest-car bonus (C9.6.1), with the bonus removed when a performance rule is failed (C3.6.2).
  - `choose_cars`: picks Car A or Car B per round (C9.7.2b) and reports the gain over always racing the better single car.
- `tests/test_outcome.py`: 13 tests on the formulas (hand-checked values), symmetry, ties, DNFs, validation, determinism and the car-choice behaviour.
- `rulebooks/stem-racing-india-nf-2025-26.yaml` and `docs/build/MASTER-PLAN.md` section 13 were added in the previous commit; this task is the first one from that plan.

## Decisions and deviations
- Worked on branch `next-wave`, not `main`, so nothing lands before the owner has reviewed it. `06-GIT-AND-HANDOFF.md` says main only.
- Knock-out assumes a 16-team bracket with four rounds, because the C9.6 points table has five outcomes. The text's round names are inconsistent (Round of 16, Round of 8, Quarter Final, Semi Final), so the code counts rounds won.
- `reaction_races` is a parameter (default 2) because C9.5 says two total times are considered while C9.1 says four races are run.
- Reaction time is drawn from a normal truncated at zero. The mean and spread are inputs, not defaults, so no invented number enters a result.
- Rounds are assumed independent. Ties count as half a win.

## Known gaps (for reviewer)
- The field model (other teams' times and the bracket opponents) is an assumption. Every result echoes `field_source`; use `synthetic` or `estimated` until real results exist.
- Formulas were checked by hand against the PDF text, not against an official worked example. Please read C9.5 and C9.6 once against `outcome.py`.
- Not wired to ORACLE, the API or the Pit Wall. The next step is to feed ORACLE's CdA posterior through `montecarlo.run` into `expected_racing_outcome` and show expected points with their spread.
- No reaction-time data yet; the reaction trainer's recorded values should replace the inputs.
