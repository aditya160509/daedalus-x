## Decisions and deviations
- Corrected the task's terminology: Ux/U∞=0.5 is a velocity-deficit surface, not a constant total-pressure-loss surface. Cp0 uses outlet static pressure as reference; it is approximately one in undisturbed flow.
- Reused VTK surface-constrained RK2 and its triangle crossing/projection, rather than duplicate its integrator. Seed sampling is repeatable and area weighted; mirroring yields about 3,000 full-model seeds.
- OpenFOAM wallShearStress is kinematic and uses the inward-to-fluid normal. Fluid traction is its negative; Pa magnitude multiplies density. Boundary drag uses p Sf_x minus wallShearStress_x times face area, divided by dynamic pressure times half reference area. No density or symmetry factor is counted twice.
- Separation flags are a heuristic near low-shear sign crossings, not a claim to distinguish separation from reattachment or prove either physically.
