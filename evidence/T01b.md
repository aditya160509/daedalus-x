# T01b: contact-patch mesh fix

**Model:** Claude Opus 5.5 · **Date:** 2026-11-01 · **Status:** review (mesh gate passes; no solver run, by design, until the new car model lands)

## Cause
The T01 v2 quick mesh failed checkMesh with 51 highly skewed faces (max 6.38 against the limit of 4). Two settings caused it:
1. **Ground offset too small.** The ground plane rose only 0.5 mm into the wheels, leaving sliver cells at the four contact patches.
2. **Snappy's limit didn't match checkMesh.** `meshQualityControls` allowed `maxBoundarySkewness 20`, so snappyHexMesh kept boundary faces that checkMesh then rejected. checkMesh counts every face above 4 as highly skewed.

## Change
- `CaseConfig.ground_offset_m`: 0.0005 → **0.0015 m**. The manifest assumption and the contact-patch note are updated to match.
- `meshQualityDict`: `maxBoundarySkewness` 20 → **4**, the same as the checkMesh gate.
- CLI: `daedalus cfd run … --mesh-only` stops after checkMesh. `--ground-offset` overrides the offset.
- `run_case(stop_after=...)`.
