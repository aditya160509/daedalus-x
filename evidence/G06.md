# G06 — All-rule margin bars with tolerance bands and points × failure ordering

**Status:** built. Software requirements of the §28 G06 entry are implemented and tested. The
normal-distribution spread remains a team-entered assumption, not measured process capability;
that limitation is printed on the panel and is unchanged by this work.

## What changed

- `src/daedalus/forge_probability.py`: `RuleRisk` now carries the rulebook `page` and the signed
  nearest-limit `margin` straight from the FORGE check, so the UI no longer has to re-derive them
  or guess a page number.
- `apps/cockpit/src/lib/api.ts`: `RuleRisk` validates `page` (positive integer) and `margin`
  (nullable) from the API.
- `apps/cockpit/src/lib/forgeMargin.ts`: new `marginBand`, `orderRiskRows`,
  `RISK_ORDER_LABELS` and `RISK_ORDER_OPTIONS`.
  - `marginBand` returns a bar only for two-sided limits with a positive declared sigma, and
    maps the nearest-limit margin and the ±1σ spread onto the allowed two-sided span in percent.
  - One-sided limits, manual rules and zero/absent sigma return `null`, so they stay listed with
    units instead of being plotted as a false zero margin.
  - `orderRiskRows` orders by probability, total points, or expected points honestly. The
    expected-points ordering requires both a penalty and a failure probability; otherwise the row
    is kept in a clearly separate unranked group.
- `apps/cockpit/src/components/pitwall/LegalPanel.tsx`: margin chart (ECharts custom series),
  ±1σ band, percent-of-span axis, rule page in the tooltip/table, saved-PNG export and an
  explicit ordering toggle. The chart caption says the spread is an independent Normal
  assumption, not correlated risk. Unavailable bands are counted and explained under the chart.
- `apps/cockpit/src/components/charts/useECharts.ts`: registers `CustomChart`.
